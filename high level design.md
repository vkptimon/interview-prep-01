## Things to remember when creating a HLD for an application

- Ask the Functional and Non-Functional requirements
- Costing limitations involved in the project
- Degree of CASP(Consistency, Availability, Scalability, Performance)

- During the interview, you need to the follow the below discussion cycle
	- Discuss
	- Think
	- Take an approach
	- Repeat until you meet the constraints
- Among the approachs made, weigh the tradeoffs for the approach and decide the one which is needed at the moment

## Things to remember when creating a LLD(Machine Coding or Object Oriented Interviews)

- Draw a class or UML diagram
- Code it all
